# BookedRadar Customer Welcome / Onboarding Email Template

**Subject:** Welcome to BookedRadar — your [Package Name] setup is ready to begin

Hi [First Name],

Welcome to BookedRadar. Your selected service is **[Package Name]** at **[Monthly Price]** [plus applicable setup fee / with setup waived].

We’ll keep the setup simple and do the technical work on our side.

Here’s what happens next:

1. **Complete the BookedRadar Quick Start** if you have not already. It gives us your business hours, service area, main services, coverage preference, and the person who should receive urgent or transferred calls.
2. **We translate your package into your business configuration.** You do not need to choose technical feature flags. We map your approved package, business rules, service area, urgency guidance, lead capture and integrations into your tenant.
3. **We show you what is active versus gated.** A feature may be included in your package but remain off until a provider, consent, credential or acceptance requirement is complete.
4. **We send your phone-forwarding instructions.** You keep your existing business number. We’ll give you the exact steps for your current phone provider and chosen coverage mode.
5. **We test before real callers use it.** We verify every customer-facing capability that will be active on day one.
6. **You make one acceptance call.** You’ll call your normal business number, give BookedRadar a test service request, and confirm that your team receives the information and any requested transfer correctly.
7. **We activate only after you approve the test.**

### Scheduling

Unless your signed service order specifically activates **RadarSchedule** or **RadarDispatch** and its scheduling acceptance test has passed, BookedRadar stays in **confirm-only scheduling**. We can collect the caller’s preferred appointment window, but your team confirms the actual appointment.

Buying a package that includes a capability does not automatically activate that capability. For example, customer-facing SMS, transcript history, web chat and live calendar booking each remain gated until their specific setup and acceptance requirements are complete.

### Security

Please do not send passwords by email. If an integration needs access, we’ll use the appropriate secure authorization or credential process.

### What we will measure

Depending on your package, your dashboards can show call activity, open opportunities, recovery activity, confirmed outcomes, revenue leaks, customer history and other package-specific signals. Estimated opportunity value is always kept separate from confirmed revenue.

We’re looking forward to helping make sure valuable calls and opportunities do not disappear when your team is busy or off the clock.
