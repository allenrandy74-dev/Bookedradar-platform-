# BookedRadar Customer Welcome / Onboarding Email Template

**Subject:** Welcome to BookedRadar — your setup is ready to begin

Hi [First Name],

Welcome to BookedRadar. We’ll keep the setup simple and do the technical work on our side.

Here’s what happens next:

1. **Complete the BookedRadar Quick Start** if you have not already. It gives us your business hours, service area, main services, coverage preference, and the person who should receive urgent or transferred calls.
2. **We build your business configuration.** We translate your answers into the call flow, service rules, urgency guidance, lead capture, and approved integrations.
3. **We send your phone-forwarding instructions.** You keep your existing business number. We’ll give you the exact steps for your current phone provider and chosen coverage mode.
4. **We test before real callers use it.** We verify the lead record, notifications, and transfer path that are enabled for your account.
5. **You make one acceptance call.** You’ll call your normal business number, give BookedRadar a test service request, and confirm that your team receives the information and any requested transfer correctly.
6. **We activate only after you approve the test.**

Your Founding Partner Pilot uses **confirm-only scheduling**: BookedRadar can collect the caller’s preferred appointment window, but your team confirms the actual appointment. Direct live scheduling or dispatch is a separate service because it requires your job durations, crew availability, travel rules, calendar/dispatch integration, and additional testing.

Please do not send passwords by email. If an integration needs access, we’ll use the appropriate secure authorization or credential process.

We’re looking forward to helping make sure valuable calls and opportunities do not disappear when your team is busy or off the clock.
