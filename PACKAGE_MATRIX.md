# BookedRadar Package Matrix

This document is the customer-facing source of truth for package positioning. Each higher package includes everything in the package below it, subject to provider, consent, and customer-specific acceptance requirements.

## Package ladder

### 1. RadarAnswer — $149/month
**Purpose:** Make sure the business answers professionally, captures the right information, and gets urgent callers to a person.

**Includes**
- AI call answering for the agreed coverage window.
- Business-specific greeting and service intake.
- English and Spanish handling.
- Caller name, callback, service need, service address, city, urgency, and preferred timing capture when applicable.
- Returning-caller memory with identity-safe verification.
- Conservative spam / solicitor screening.
- Human escalation / transfer using the approved transfer path.
- Transfer companion summary when configured.
- Basic CRM/contact/task handoff when a supported customer system is connected.
- Confirm-only scheduling: preferred date/time captured for the team to confirm.
- Knowledge Gap Radar for business questions the AI cannot answer safely.
- Owner visibility into call activity.
- Searchable transcript history when the customer explicitly approves transcript retention.

**Primary benefits**
- Fewer missed calls.
- Less time spent answering routine calls.
- Cleaner lead information for office staff or on-call technicians.
- Faster escalation of urgent calls.
- More consistent customer experience after hours or during overflow.
- Lower-risk AI behavior because BookedRadar records unknown questions instead of guessing.

**Does not include**
- Automated missed-opportunity recovery campaigns.
- Web chat.
- Two-way customer SMS continuity.
- Membership renewal workflows.
- No-show prevention.
- Live calendar booking.
- Full dispatch.

**Best fit**
Businesses that mainly want professional call coverage and lead capture without a larger automation program.

### 2. RadarRecover — $497/month
**Founding Partner:** $397/month, setup waived.

**Purpose:** Everything in RadarAnswer, plus actively identify and recover opportunities the business would otherwise lose.

**Everything in RadarAnswer, plus**
- Missed-call recovery workflows.
- Web-lead follow-up / fast-response workflows.
- Stale-estimate recovery.
- Cancellation recovery / reschedule workflows.
- Dormant-opportunity review subject to consent rules.
- Embeddable web chat when activated for the customer's approved website.
- In-call transactional texting when approved messaging is active.
- Two-way SMS continuity when approved messaging is active.
- RadarProof.
- Revenue Leak Radar.
- Owner Daily Brief / Command Center.
- Opportunity Timeline.
- Customer 360.
- Review Radar.
- Call-history dashboard.
- Cross-channel opportunity context across supported voice, SMS, web, CRM and recovery flows.

**Primary benefits**
- Recover revenue from missed calls, stale estimates, cancellations and neglected leads.
- Give owners a daily view of where money is leaking.
- Show which opportunities were actually recovered.
- Reduce dependence on employees remembering to follow up.
- Keep customer context together instead of scattered across voicemail, text, web forms and CRM tasks.
- Make the monthly fee easier to justify through measurable recovered value.

**Does not include**
- Automated membership-renewal program.
- Automated appointment-reminder / No-Show Guard messaging.
- Live calendar booking.
- Technician assignment, routing, or full dispatch.
- Pricing or quote authority.
- Payment collection.

**Best fit**
Home-service businesses that already get valuable inbound leads but lose revenue through missed calls, slow follow-up, stale estimates or cancellations.

### 3. RadarGrow — $697/month
**Founding Partner:** $597/month, setup waived.

**Purpose:** Everything in RadarRecover, plus help protect recurring revenue and strengthen the customer's long-term customer base.

**Everything in RadarRecover, plus**
- Membership Radar for upcoming service-agreement renewals.
- No-Show Guard for approved appointment reminders and change requests.
- Expanded review-intelligence workflows.
- Earlier-slot / cancellation-backfill intelligence.
- Growth-oriented Owner Brief signals for renewals, review-ready jobs and retention opportunities.

**Primary benefits**
- Protect recurring maintenance / membership revenue.
- Reduce no-shows and wasted technician capacity.
- Make better use of canceled appointment slots.
- Build review volume without blindly asking unhappy or unresolved customers.
- Improve retention and repeat business, not just lead capture.
- Give owners more predictable recurring-revenue visibility.

**Activation note**
Membership intelligence can operate without customer-facing messaging. Automated reminders or SMS require an approved tenant messaging provider, consent/compliance configuration and acceptance testing.

**Does not include**
- Live calendar writes unless separately upgraded to RadarSchedule.
- Technician routing or dispatch optimization.
- Pricing / quoting authority.
- Payment collection.

**Best fit**
Businesses with maintenance agreements, recurring service, meaningful appointment volume, and a strong focus on retention and reputation.

### 4. RadarSchedule — $897/month
**Founding Partner:** $797/month, setup waived.

**Purpose:** Everything in RadarGrow, plus simple live booking into an approved connected calendar.

**Everything in RadarGrow, plus**
- Separately approved live calendar booking.
- Real provider free/busy checks.
- Concrete availability windows rather than invented availability.
- Slot re-check immediately before writing the appointment.
- Provider booking ID returned as booking evidence.
- Customer-specific duration / booking rules supported by the connected adapter.

**Primary benefits**
- Convert more inbound demand directly into booked appointments.
- Reduce office scheduling workload.
- Shorten time from first contact to confirmed appointment.
- Reduce double-booking risk by checking availability immediately before insertion.
- Keep booking outcomes connected to RadarProof and recovery data.

**Activation requirements**
RadarSchedule is not turned on merely because the customer buys the package. Live booking requires:
- approved scheduling scope,
- signed agreement reference,
- supported calendar credentials,
- customer-specific booking rules,
- controlled booking tests,
- and a live acceptance test.

Until those pass, the tenant remains confirm-only.

**Does not include**
- Technician assignment.
- Route optimization.
- Travel-time decisions.
- Skill-based field dispatch.
- Full-time scheduler / dispatcher operation.

**Best fit**
Businesses where a straightforward appointment can be booked into a calendar without complex technician/territory logic.

### 5. RadarDispatch — Custom pricing
**Purpose:** Full operational scheduling and field-service dispatch.

**Everything in RadarSchedule, plus an approved custom operating scope that may include**
- Technician / crew assignment.
- Skill and certification matching.
- Territory and service-area rules.
- Travel-time and drive-time buffers.
- Job-duration rules by service type.
- Emergency priority and displacement rules.
- Capacity protection.
- Maximum jobs per technician / crew.
- Lunch, shop, parts-pickup and other scheduling buffers.
- Reschedule / override authority rules.
- Customer-specific dispatch system integration.
- Technician briefing / Customer 360 context.
- Future route and capacity optimization capabilities as validated.

**Primary benefits**
- Replace or reduce manual dispatch coordination.
- Improve technician utilization.
- Reduce unnecessary drive time.
- Put the right technician on the right job.
- Protect emergency capacity.
- Scale scheduling volume without adding equivalent office labor.

**Why custom**
Dispatch rules vary dramatically by business. BookedRadar will not sell a generic "AI dispatch" promise without understanding the customer's technicians, geography, durations, policies, integrations and override rules.

**Best fit**
Larger or higher-volume service companies with meaningful scheduling/dispatch complexity.

## Simple escalation story

**RadarAnswer:** Answer the opportunity.  
**RadarRecover:** Recover the opportunity.  
**RadarGrow:** Grow and retain the customer.  
**RadarSchedule:** Book the work.  
**RadarDispatch:** Optimize the field operation.

## Sales rule

Never sell a higher tier by listing more technology. Explain the additional business outcome:

- Answer → fewer missed calls and less office interruption.
- Recover → less lost revenue and provable ROI.
- Grow → stronger recurring revenue, reviews and customer retention.
- Schedule → more leads converted directly into booked work.
- Dispatch → more efficient field capacity and less scheduling labor.

## Activation rule

A package can include a capability without that capability being immediately active. Provider-dependent, consent-dependent, or higher-risk functions remain gated until customer-specific setup and acceptance testing pass. Sales must describe them as "included once activated" rather than "already active" until that gate is complete.
