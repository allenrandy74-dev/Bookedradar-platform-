# BookedRadar Quick Start

This is the customer-facing onboarding script/form outline.

## Step 1 — Tell us about your business

**Required**
- Your name
- Business name
- Business type/trade
- Best email and phone number
- Service area
- Services you want BookedRadar to recognize
- Normal business hours

## Step 2 — Tell us how you want calls handled

**Required**
- Coverage: after-hours / overflow-no-answer / broader coverage
- Who should receive urgent or transferred calls? Name + mobile number

**Optional**
- Current phone provider
- What situations do you consider urgent?

## Step 3 — Tell us where work gets tracked

**Optional**
- Where do you keep customers and leads today?
- Do you want BookedRadar to capture a preferred appointment window, or connect to live booking?
- Preferred email address for customer replies
- Website
- Anything else we should know

## What happens next

BookedRadar will:
1. Build your business configuration.
2. Send you exact phone-forwarding instructions for your provider.
3. Connect only the systems you choose.
4. Test CRM/email/SMS features before enabling them.
5. Run one acceptance call with you.
6. Activate the agreed coverage after you approve the test.

You keep your existing business number unless we specifically agree otherwise.
