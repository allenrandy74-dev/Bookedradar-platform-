# BookedRadar — One-Page Business Explainer

## Stop losing good opportunities because nobody could answer or follow up fast enough.

BookedRadar is an AI-powered call answering and revenue-recovery system for service businesses. It helps capture the customer, preserve the context, get urgent calls to a person, recover opportunities that would otherwise be forgotten, and show the owner what happened.

### What a caller experiences

1. BookedRadar answers using the business's approved identity and rules.
2. It collects the caller's name, callback number, service need, address, urgency and preferred timing when applicable.
3. It asks one question at a time and keeps the conversation natural.
4. When the caller needs a person, BookedRadar sends the receiving person a summary, provides a short hold message, and uses the proven transfer path.
5. If BookedRadar does not know a business-specific policy, it records the question for review rather than inventing an answer.

### What the owner gets

- Fewer missed opportunities.
- Cleaner lead information for the team.
- Recovery workflows for missed or neglected opportunities in packages that include them.
- CRM/contact/task handoff when a supported system is connected.
- RadarProof and Owner Brief visibility into activity and recovered opportunities.
- Human escalation and fail-closed behavior for unsupported or unapproved actions.
- A system that can expand from answering → recovery → growth → live scheduling → custom dispatch without pretending every capability is active on day one.

### Simple onboarding

The customer provides six Quick Start answers: business name/type, service area, services, business hours, escalation contact, and desired coverage mode.

BookedRadar handles the technical translation. The customer keeps the existing business number unless separately agreed. Phone forwarding, CRM, messaging, scheduling and other integrations are activated only after the applicable customer-specific setup and acceptance tests pass.

### Important operating boundaries

- Confirm-only scheduling is the default. A preferred time is not a confirmed appointment.
- Live booking and full dispatch require separate approval and testing.
- Audio recording is off by default.
- Provider-, consent- and acceptance-dependent features stay gated until ready.
- Published package pricing does not promise unlimited usage.
- RadarProof separates diagnostic opportunity value from confirmed revenue.
- BookedRadar does not guarantee revenue or replace emergency services, licensed tradespeople or the customer's field judgment.
