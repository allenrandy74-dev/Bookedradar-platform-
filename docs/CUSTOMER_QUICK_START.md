# BookedRadar Quick Start — Customer Experience

## Promise
**Tell us how your business works. We handle the setup.**

Target customer time: **10 minutes or less** for the first pass.

The long master onboarding checklist is for BookedRadar internally. Customers should not be presented with it.

## Step 1 — Your business
Ask only:
1. What is your business name?
2. What kind of work do you do?
3. What areas do you serve?
4. What are your normal business hours?
5. What are your main services?

A website is optional. If supplied, BookedRadar can use it to prepare information for the customer to review instead of asking them to retype it.

## Step 2 — How should we help?
6. When would you like BookedRadar to help with calls?
   - After hours
   - When nobody answers
   - When your team is busy
   - A combination
   - I'm not sure — recommend a setup
7. Who should we contact when a caller needs a person?
8. In your business, what usually counts as urgent?

Do not ask the customer to configure phone technology during this step.

## Step 3 — How do you work today?
9. Where do you keep customer and lead information?
   - A business software/app
   - A spreadsheet
   - Paper/notes
   - We don't really have a system
   - Not sure
10. Do you schedule appointments while speaking with customers?
   - Yes
   - No
   - Sometimes
11. Anything else you want BookedRadar to know?

## Step 4 — Review
Show a plain-language summary and a single action:
**Looks Good — Prepare My Setup**

## Behind the scenes
BookedRadar translates the answers into a tenant draft, determines which connections actually apply, asks only for missing required information, and runs readiness and synthetic tests before activation.

## Action labels
Every remaining setup item must be one of:
- **We'll handle it**
- **We need this from you**
- **You need to do this — Show me how**

Technical implementation details stay hidden unless the customer deliberately opens advanced details.

## Activation
Customer-facing progress uses business language:
- Business setup
- Call handling
- Customer follow-up
- Notifications
- Test BookedRadar
- Ready to go live

No customer traffic is activated until applicable readiness and acceptance tests pass.
