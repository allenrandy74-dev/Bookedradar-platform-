# BookedRadar Customer FAQ

## What is BookedRadar?
BookedRadar is a customer-response system for service businesses. It can handle selected incoming calls, gather useful lead information, and route or organize follow-up based on the business's rules.

## Do I have to replace my business phone number?
The intended setup is to preserve the customer's existing public number whenever the selected phone provider/routing setup supports forwarding or overflow routing.

## When can BookedRadar answer?
The business chooses. Common starting options are after-hours, unanswered calls, busy/overflow calls, or selected hours.

## What information can it collect?
A tenant can be configured to collect caller name, callback number, exact service address, service need, urgency, preferred timing and notes.

## Can a caller reach a person?
Human escalation can be configured. The exact transfer and notification behavior is tested for the customer before activation.

## Where does the information go?
That depends on how the business works today. BookedRadar can be configured around supported customer-management and notification systems. The onboarding process asks in plain language where the business currently keeps leads.

## Do I need special software?
Not necessarily. The Quick Start asks about the tools already used. BookedRadar determines which connections are needed and provides simple instructions only for customer-required actions.

## Does BookedRadar quote prices?
Only if the customer explicitly approves pricing rules and the tenant is configured for it. Otherwise it should not invent or promise prices.

## Can it schedule appointments?
The $497 pilot collects appointment requests and preferred times; your team confirms availability. Direct calendar booking, full-time scheduling and dispatch are separately scoped and priced. These services require approved scheduling rules, supported integrations and acceptance testing before activation.

## What happens with emergencies?
The customer defines urgent and emergency handling rules. BookedRadar must follow approved safety/escalation boundaries and must not diagnose hazardous situations.

## How long does setup take?
The initial Quick Start is designed to take about 10 minutes. Technical configuration and testing happen afterward, with the customer shown only the steps that require their participation.

## Can I test it before customers use it?
Yes. Automated/synthetic checks come first, followed by a controlled customer acceptance test before activation.

## What happens after launch?
Early pilots are monitored closely. BookedRadar reviews lead completeness, transfers, integration delivery and failures, then uses attributable outcomes for RadarProof reporting where applicable.
