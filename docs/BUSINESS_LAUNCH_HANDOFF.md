# BookedRadar Business Launch — Owner/Admin Handoff

These are the items that genuinely depend on business formation or owner action. Technical/product work should continue without waiting on them.

## Mailing address
When the UPS mailbox is opened:
- Record the exact commercial mailing format UPS provides.
- Use the same approved business address consistently where appropriate across business records.
- Confirm whether the location qualifies for each provider's address requirements before using it as a registered/legal address; mailing and registered-agent requirements are not always the same.

## LLC
When formation is approved:
- Save the filed formation document and state confirmation.
- Confirm the exact legal entity name.
- Record the effective/filing date.
- Keep the public-facing BookedRadar brand name and legal entity name distinction clear if they differ.

## EIN
After the LLC is finalized:
- Apply through the IRS using the final legal entity information.
- Save the EIN confirmation letter securely.
- Do not put the EIN in source code, public documents, or ordinary customer-facing materials.

## Business banking
After EIN:
- Open the selected business checking account.
- Keep business income/expenses separated from personal activity.
- Connect billing/payment systems only after the business account is active.
- Record authorized signer and accounting access intentionally.

## Before first paid customer
- Final customer service agreement approved.
- Payment/billing workflow tested.
- Refund/cancellation terms clear.
- W-9/business tax information ready when requested.
- Business insurance needs reviewed.
- Privacy/data-processing obligations reviewed for the actual services sold.
- Customer support/escalation contact established.

## Owner handoff rule
Never block product testing on an administrative item that is not actually required for the test. Never accept payment or sign a customer contract until the business/payment/legal prerequisites for that transaction are in place.
