# BookedRadar — Customer One-Pager Copy

## Never let a good customer inquiry disappear into a missed call.

BookedRadar helps service businesses respond when the team cannot get to the phone. It can answer selected calls, gather the information your team needs, identify urgency, and organize the follow-up — using rules built around your business.

### You choose when BookedRadar helps
- After hours
- When nobody answers
- When your team is busy
- A combination that fits your operation

### Built around your business
BookedRadar can be configured to capture:
- Caller name and callback number
- Exact service address
- What the customer needs
- How urgent it is
- Preferred timing
- Additional notes

When a caller needs a person, BookedRadar can follow the escalation process configured and tested for your business.

### You do not need to learn new technology to get started
Tell us how your business works. We handle the technical setup.

### Start small
For many businesses, a controlled pilot can begin with after-hours and/or unanswered calls. Your existing workflow stays central while BookedRadar proves its value.
