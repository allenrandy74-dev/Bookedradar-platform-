# BookedRadar Founding Customer Sales Playbook

## Core promise
BookedRadar helps service businesses respond when their team cannot get to the phone, capture the information needed for follow-up, and hand important opportunities to a real person when appropriate.

Do not promise revenue, perfect call handling, or capabilities that have not passed the tenant-specific acceptance test.

## First conversation
Keep discovery short:
1. What happens today when a customer calls and nobody can answer?
2. Are after-hours calls handled differently?
3. How does the team receive and follow up with missed-call information?
4. Which calls need a person immediately?
5. Where does the business keep customer/lead information today?
6. Would the owner prefer to start with after-hours, unanswered calls, or a small pilot?

## Demonstration
Show only capabilities that are production-verified for the prospect's proposed configuration:
- Business-specific greeting
- Caller name and callback number
- Exact service address
- Service needed
- Urgency
- Preferred timing
- Human escalation/transfer when configured
- CRM/task creation when configured
- Notifications when verified and configured
- Pleasant close and handoff

## Simple close
If the business is interested:
1. Send the BookedRadar Quick Start onboarding link.
2. Customer spends about 10 minutes describing the business.
3. BookedRadar prepares the configuration.
4. We show only remaining actions required from the customer.
5. We run synthetic tests.
6. Customer makes a controlled acceptance call.
7. Customer approves the pilot.
8. Activate only the agreed routing scope.

## Founding-customer pilot
Recommended scope: after-hours and/or unanswered/overflow calls first.

Before offering a commercial price, use the approved BookedRadar pricing sheet. Do not improvise discounts or contractual terms.

## Objection handling
### “I already have voicemail.”
BookedRadar's value proposition is interactive intake and structured follow-up rather than simply recording a message. Demonstrate the difference; do not disparage voicemail.

### “I don't want AI upsetting customers.”
Offer a controlled test using the business's own greeting, rules, FAQs and escalation process. The owner experiences it before activation.

### “I don't want to change my number.”
Where technically supported, design the pilot around forwarding/routing from the existing business number rather than requiring a public-number change.

### “What if it doesn't know the answer?”
Configure approved knowledge and boundaries. When uncertain or outside scope, it should capture the request or escalate rather than invent an answer.

### “Can it book jobs?”
Use only the booking mode verified for that tenant. Confirm-only and live-booking are distinct capabilities.

### “What if something breaks?”
Each tenant must have an escalation/rollback path and pass readiness testing before customer traffic is enabled.

## Sales rule
The sales process must never outrun the production readiness matrix. A feature can be demonstrated or sold only when its applicable configuration and acceptance tests have passed.

## Approved pilot boundary — September 24, 2026
The $497/month Founding Partner Pilot covers the agreed call-answering, lead-capture,
routing, follow-up and appointment-request workflow. The setup fee is waived for the
first five qualified founding partners. It is not an unlimited-call or unlimited-usage
plan. Volume, coverage hours, integrations and any separate usage costs must be stated
in the customer order before signing or payment.

An appointment request is not a confirmed booking. Capture the preferred time and tell
the caller the business will confirm availability. Full-time scheduling, direct calendar
booking, technician assignment, travel-aware routing, dispatch and schedule optimization
require a separate scope, setup quote, recurring price and customer-specific acceptance.
No advanced-scheduling price or setup-fee waiver has been approved.
