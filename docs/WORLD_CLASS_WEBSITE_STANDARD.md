## Lifecycle
Headline: **Answering is only the beginning.**

CAPTURE — Structured service/address/urgency/timing intake.
ESCALATE — Human requests, safety-sensitive and unusual situations get an escape path.
RECOVER — Missed calls, stale estimates and approved follow-up become actionable.
PROVE — Separate activity, engagement, recovery and confirmed revenue.

## Comparison
Headline: **Not another AI receptionist.**

Typical answering automation:
- answers calls
- gives basic information
- books when possible
- reports activity counts

BookedRadar:
- captures structured demand
- escalates exceptions to humans
- recovers missed and stale opportunities
- uses trade and customer-approved business rules
- tracks outcomes, not just conversations

## RadarProof
Headline: **Proof should be harder to fake than a dashboard number.**

Evidence ladder:
Captured -> Engaged -> Booked/Accepted -> Recovered -> Revenue Confirmed

Trust copy:
Estimated opportunity value is not confirmed revenue. Incomplete and failed outcomes belong in the review too. A credible pilot shows the whole picture.

Never present illustrative/demo numbers as customer results.

## Proof Pilot
Headline: **Start narrow. Test hard. Expand only after proof.**

Qualified pilot:
- up to 14 days
- up to 25 real calls
- after-hours OR overflow
- human escalation preserved
- customer-specific acceptance testing first
- RadarProof review at end
- no automatic paid conversion
- no automatic scope expansion

CTA: See if BookedRadar is a fit

Initial form fields only:
1. Name
2. Business
3. Trade
4. Email
5. Phone
Require email OR phone.
Collect workflow detail after initial interest.

## Visual system
Premium operational/industrial SaaS, not generic "AI neon robot" styling.
- deep navy / ink base
- white and cool neutral surfaces
- restrained blue/cyan accents
- generous whitespace
- strong typography and short copy
- real product/radar UI visuals
- no stock call-center photos
- no fake logos/testimonials
- mobile-first live-demo call buttons
- accessible contrast and tap targets

## Social proof policy
Until real customers exist:
- do not fabricate testimonials
- do not invent logos
- use product proof, live demos, acceptance rigor, transparent pricing and RadarProof methodology

After customer permission:
- add named case studies
- quantify only verified outcomes
- show trade, starting workflow, pilot scope, measured result, and what did NOT work / was corrected when relevant

## Conversion measurement
Track:
- live demo CTA clicks by trade
- Proof Pilot form submissions
- qualified inquiries
- demo-to-pilot rate
- pilot-to-paid rate
- time to first value
- pricing-page engagement where privacy-compliant

Do not optimize for page views alone.

## Native Wix preservation
Preserve:
- existing site ID
- Wix Forms / CRM
- privacy, terms, security pages
- sitemap / robots / Search Console work
- existing domain
- existing redirects unless deliberately replaced
- SEO metadata that remains accurate

## Publish acceptance
Before calling the homepage complete:
- desktop visual review
- iPhone visual review
- every CTA tested
- every demo number tested
- Proof Pilot form test creates one CRM contact/task only
- pricing matches production commercial configuration
- no stale "pilot" language that confuses paid Founding Partner pricing with the capped Proof Pilot
- privacy/terms/security links work
- page speed acceptable
- no horizontal mobile overflow
