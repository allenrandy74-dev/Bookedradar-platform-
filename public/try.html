What BookedRadar is—and isn't

It is: an operational layer for answering, structured lead capture, human routing, follow-up and recovery workflows.

It isn't: a promise that automation should handle every customer situation. Complex, high-value, safety-sensitive and human-requested conversations need a clean escalation path.
