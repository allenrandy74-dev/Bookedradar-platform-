STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Know what happens before you trust us with customers.

Is this just an AI receptionist?

No. Call handling is one input. The platform is built around capture, human escalation, recovery and outcome proof.

Does BookedRadar identify itself as a virtual assistant?

Our live demos do. Customer wording is configured transparently and tested before activation.

Does BookedRadar replace my office staff?

No. It is designed to support the team, handle repetitive intake/follow-up, and get out of the way when a person is needed.

Will it book appointments automatically?

Not by default. Confirm-only is the safe starting mode. Live scheduling requires separate scope, integration and acceptance testing.

What happens during a Proof Pilot?

We configure your rules, acceptance-test the experience, start with selected after-hours or overflow traffic, cap the pilot at up to 14 days or 25 real calls, and review the evidence with you.

Can I stop the pilot?

Yes. The pilot is designed to be controlled and reversible. There is no automatic paid conversion.

What happens if an integration fails?

Required integrations are acceptance-tested before activation. Material handoff failures can pause a pilot for review rather than silently continuing as if delivery succeeded.
