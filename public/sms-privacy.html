Privacy Policy

This policy covers BookedRadar’s internal transfer-summary SMS program, operated by Randy Allen.

Information used

We use the designated recipient’s mobile number, opt-in and opt-out messages, messaging records and delivery status to operate the program and manage consent. Transfer messages may contain caller information collected during intake, including name, service request, urgency, preferred timing, address and callback number.

Purpose and sharing

Information is used to prepare the authorized human recipient for a requested call transfer, troubleshoot delivery and maintain consent records. Hosting, telephony, messaging and AI service providers process information to deliver the service.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Choices and retention

Reply STOP to opt out. Contact randy@bookedradar.com to request access, correction or deletion of your information. We retain records as reasonably needed for service operation, consent management, troubleshooting and legal obligations. Some records may need to be retained to honor opt-outs.
