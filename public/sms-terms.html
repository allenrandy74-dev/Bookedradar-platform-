SMS Terms

BookedRadar, operated by Randy Allen, provides automated operational SMS briefings to its designated human transfer recipient. Messages identify the business being represented and available caller details before a requested human handoff. This program is not a marketing subscription and does not enroll the inbound caller.

Text START to (409) 257-4186 to consent to recurring BookedRadar transfer-summary messages. Message frequency varies with transfer activity. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of purchasing a service.

Reply STOP to cancel. Reply HELP for assistance, or email randy@bookedradar.com. You can opt in again by texting START.

Delivery and use

Messages are subject to carrier approval, network availability and delivery delays. A send request or message delivery does not guarantee that the human has read the summary or that a call will connect. The SMS program is not an emergency service.

Transfer summaries contain information provided during intake and may be incomplete. Recipients should confirm necessary details with the caller and protect caller information from unauthorized disclosure.
