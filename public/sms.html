BOOKEDRADAR

Transfer briefings for the person answering the call

This SMS program sends internal operational briefings to the BookedRadar owner designated to receive human call transfers. It does not enroll inbound callers in messaging.

Opt in by text

To consent to receive recurring automated BookedRadar transfer-summary texts at your mobile number, text START to (409) 257-4186.

Messages identify the business represented and may include the caller’s name, service request, urgency, preferred timing, service address and callback number when available. Frequency varies with transfer requests. Message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP to stop messages. Reply HELP for assistance, or email randy@bookedradar.com. SMS consent is not a condition of purchasing a service.
