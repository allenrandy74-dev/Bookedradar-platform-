## First outreach rule

Do not lead with "AI receptionist." Lead with the operational problem:

1. A valuable call arrives when the office is busy or closed.
2. BookedRadar answers on the business's behalf.
3. It captures the service need, address, urgency and preferred timing.
4. It transfers true urgent/human-request calls using the proven handoff path.
5. It records the opportunity so it does not disappear into voicemail.
6. The business keeps its existing number and staff.

Offer the Founding Partner Pilot only after confirming fit. Do not promise live scheduling/dispatch unless a separate scheduling scope has been approved.
