# BookedRadar Customer Success Review Templates

## Principle
Every review should answer three questions:
1. Is the service working reliably?
2. Is the customer receiving useful opportunities and outcomes?
3. What is the smallest next improvement that would create more value?

Do not use a value review to hide a reliability problem. Reliability/readiness issues come first.
