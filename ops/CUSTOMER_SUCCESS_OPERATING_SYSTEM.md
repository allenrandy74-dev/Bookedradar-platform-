# BookedRadar Customer Success Operating System

## Objective
Customers should understand BookedRadar's value before they have to ask, and BookedRadar should identify risk before a customer decides to leave.

## Ownership
Every active customer must have:
- an accountable BookedRadar owner,
- current escalation contact,
- package/service scope,
- enabled/gated capability list,
- last acceptance-test date,
- next value-review date.

## Lifecycle

### Day 0 — Activation
Verify:
- customer-specific acceptance call,
- correct business identity,
- approved phone-forwarding mode,
- human escalation,
- lead delivery/CRM,
- enabled notifications,
- known fallback/reversal instructions.

Send/record a concise go-live summary.

### Day 1 — First-value check
Review:
- calls handled,
- incomplete/problem calls,
- transfer results,
- lead delivery,
- any knowledge gaps,
- any caller friction.

Fix configuration issues before they become habits.

### Day 7 — Quality review
Review:
- call quality trends,
- repeated caller questions,
- urgency/service classification,
- customer response to leads,
- recovery workflow performance,
- support issues.

Convert real Knowledge Gap findings into approved tenant guidance.

### Day 30 — Value review
RadarProof should distinguish:
- calls/opportunities captured,
- recovered opportunities,
- accepted/booked outcomes,
- confirmed revenue,
- estimated opportunity value,
- transfers/escalations,
- unresolved gaps.

Never label estimated opportunity value as confirmed revenue.

## Customer health signals
Flag risk when any are true:
- repeated support contacts for the same issue,
- declining call volume unexpectedly,
- repeated incomplete calls,
- frequent transfer failures,
- unresolved configuration gaps,
- customer has not reviewed value outcomes,
- key integration is degraded,
- customer indicates they are bypassing BookedRadar,
- billing/service scope confusion exists.

## Retention response
For an at-risk customer:
1. Identify the concrete failure/value gap.
2. Correct configuration or reliability issue first.
3. Quantify what BookedRadar has actually delivered.
4. Do not mask dissatisfaction with discounts before understanding cause.
5. Define one measurable recovery action and review date.

## Support quality metrics
Track:
- first-response time,
- time to useful diagnosis,
- time to resolution,
- repeat incidents,
- reopen rate,
- customer-impacting incidents,
- proactive vs customer-reported detection,
- acceptance-test pass rate after changes.

## Customer communication standard
Support responses should be:
- specific,
- calm,
- accountable,
- free of unnecessary technical jargon,
- explicit about what BookedRadar is doing,
- explicit about what the customer needs to do, if anything.

Never force customers to troubleshoot SIP/webhooks/API details that BookedRadar can own.
