# BookedRadar LLC — Public Terms Source Draft

> Operational source draft for counsel review and publication. This file is intended to keep the public Terms aligned with the current BookedRadar package, billing, privacy and activation model. It is not a substitute for legal review.

**Proposed effective date:** September 25, 2026  
**Operator:** BookedRadar LLC  
**Contact:** randy@bookedradar.com

## 1. Website and service information

BookedRadar LLC ("BookedRadar") provides software-assisted call answering, lead capture, revenue-recovery, follow-up, reporting and, when separately approved and activated, scheduling or dispatch capabilities for businesses.

Website content, audits, demonstrations, sample dashboards, illustrative metrics and scenarios are provided for business evaluation. Examples are not representations of actual customer results unless explicitly labeled otherwise.

## 2. Customer service order controls the purchased scope

A customer's selected package, enabled channels, setup obligations, approved integrations, direct/provider costs, service start date and any customer-specific terms are documented in the applicable Service Order or other signed agreement.

If a signed customer-specific Service Order conflicts with general website descriptions, the signed Service Order controls for that customer.

Buying or selecting a package does not automatically activate a provider-, consent-, credential-, calendar-, messaging- or acceptance-dependent feature. Those capabilities remain gated until their applicable setup and acceptance requirements pass.

## 3. Packages and activation boundaries

BookedRadar may offer packages including RadarAnswer, RadarRecover, RadarGrow, RadarSchedule and separately scoped RadarDispatch services.

- **RadarAnswer** focuses on answering, information capture and approved escalation.
- **RadarRecover** adds approved recovery workflows and related reporting.
- **RadarGrow** adds approved retention/growth workflows where the required channels and customer permissions are active.
- **RadarSchedule** may include live booking only after the customer's supported calendar or scheduling integration, rules and authorization are configured and acceptance-tested.
- **RadarDispatch** is a custom scope requiring customer-specific operating rules, systems, capacity and acceptance testing.

Unless live scheduling has been separately approved and activated, BookedRadar remains **confirm-only**: it may collect a preferred date/time window, but it does not promise or write a confirmed appointment.

## 4. Billing, renewal and cancellation

Unless a signed Service Order states otherwise:

- recurring service is billed monthly in advance;
- service is month-to-month;
- the subscription continues until canceled;
- cancellation normally takes effect at the end of the current paid billing period;
- amounts already paid for a service period are not automatically prorated or refunded after service for that period has begun, except where required by law or expressly agreed in writing;
- applicable standard setup fees are stated in the Service Order;
- an approved Founding Partner may receive a waived setup fee and promotional monthly rate as stated in the signed Service Order;
- a Founding Partner promotional rate applies while the applicable founding subscription remains continuously active unless the Service Order states otherwise.

Approved third-party/provider costs, usage-based charges or customer-specific integration costs may be billed separately only when the Service Order or another written agreement identifies them.

Published package pricing does not promise unlimited calls, messages, AI usage, storage, integrations or provider usage. If actual volume or complexity materially exceeds the agreed assumptions, BookedRadar will seek an agreed scope or pricing change before imposing new recurring overage charges.

## 5. Payment status and service changes

BookedRadar may pause new outbound automation, new feature activation or other non-emergency service functions when payment is unresolved, subject to the signed agreement. BookedRadar will seek to avoid abrupt changes to active call routing that could create an unsafe or misleading customer experience and will use an orderly handoff where reasonably possible.

## 6. Communications and consent-dependent features

Voice, SMS, email, web-chat, reactivation, reminder and other communication capabilities are enabled only when included in the purchased scope and their applicable provider, sender identity, consent/opt-out and acceptance requirements are complete.

Customers are responsible for providing accurate customer-specific policies, permissions and consent records required for their workflows. BookedRadar supports the agreed technical controls, including supported opt-out and suppression handling.

## 7. Call recordings and transcripts

Audio recording is **off by default** and is separate from transcript history.

Transcript retention may be enabled only for a customer deployment that approves that configuration. Audio recording should not be enabled unless the applicable disclosure, consent and retention requirements have been intentionally addressed.

Customer-specific retention settings may be documented in the Service Order or deployment configuration.

## 8. Safety, emergencies and professional judgment

BookedRadar is not an emergency service and is not a substitute for licensed tradespeople, emergency responders, qualified field personnel, legal advice or professional technical diagnosis.

Customers remain responsible for their emergency procedures, field-service decisions, technician safety, availability, pricing, estimates, repairs and other professional or operational judgments. BookedRadar may escalate safety-sensitive, unusual, disputed or requested-human situations rather than forcing them through automation.

## 9. Customer responsibilities

Customers are responsible for providing accurate and current business hours, service areas, service categories, escalation contacts, safety rules, pricing/policy information and any other business-specific guidance used by the service.

Customers must promptly inform BookedRadar of material changes and must not instruct BookedRadar to misrepresent pricing, availability, credentials, confirmed appointments or other business facts.

Customers are also responsible for maintaining any customer-owned phone, CRM, calendar, dispatch or other systems needed for their selected integrations.

## 10. Third-party services

Telephony, messaging, email, CRM, calendar, scheduling, AI, hosting, payment and other capabilities may rely on third-party providers. Those providers may have separate terms, fees, limits, outages, authorization requirements and technical behavior.

BookedRadar does not guarantee the continuous availability or behavior of an outside provider.

## 11. Reporting and no guarantee of business results

RadarProof and related reporting may distinguish captured opportunities, recovery activity, booking outcomes, estimated opportunity value and confirmed revenue.

Estimated opportunity value is diagnostic and is not confirmed revenue. BookedRadar does not guarantee revenue, bookings, lead quality, customer decisions, estimate acceptance, financing approval, technician availability or any other business outcome.

## 12. Privacy and data handling

BookedRadar's public Privacy Notice describes the categories of information the service may process, the purposes for which it is used, communications practices, retention concepts and provider categories.

Customer deployments may require additional disclosures or customer-specific terms based on the channels, integrations, jurisdictions and workflows used.

## 13. Acceptable use

BookedRadar must not be configured or used to impersonate people, misrepresent pricing or availability, falsely claim confirmed appointments, collect unnecessary sensitive information, bypass consent or opt-out requirements, or send communications contrary to applicable rules or the signed customer scope.

## 14. Service changes

BookedRadar may improve or modify the service over time. Material changes to a customer's purchased scope, recurring price or separately agreed obligations should be handled through the applicable customer agreement, renewal or written amendment rather than silently changing the customer's approved scope.

## 15. Contact

Questions about these Terms can be sent to **randy@bookedradar.com**.
