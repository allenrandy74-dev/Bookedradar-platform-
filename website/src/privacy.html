Privacy

Privacy & communications

Effective September 24, 2026. This notice explains how BookedRadar handles information used to evaluate, provide, secure and improve its revenue-recovery services.

Information we may collect

We may receive business contact information, service-request details, audit responses, website-form or chat submissions, communications with BookedRadar, and information supplied by a pilot customer through authorized integrations. Depending on the service configuration, this may include caller name, callback number, email, service address or city, service need, urgency, preferred appointment window, estimate status, booking status, call or message history, transcript text, CRM identifiers, and attribution data.

How we use information

We use information to respond to inquiries, operate requested voice, messaging, web-chat and recovery workflows, create follow-up tasks, route or escalate opportunities, support customer-approved scheduling, preserve conversation context across enabled channels, measure outcomes, provide RadarProof reporting, identify knowledge gaps, secure the service, troubleshoot failures and improve BookedRadar.

Automation and human escalation

BookedRadar may use automated systems to assist with lead intake and follow-up. The system is designed to hand off safety-sensitive, unusual, high-value, disputed or requested-human situations rather than forcing them through automation. It is not intended to diagnose hazardous conditions or replace emergency services.

Communications

Transactional communications may continue a request or authorized customer workflow across enabled channels such as voice, SMS, email and web chat. Marketing or reactivation communications should only be sent when the customer record and operating policy permit them. Recognized opt-outs are treated as suppression instructions for future automated outreach through the applicable workflow.

Sharing

Information may be processed by service providers that support hosting, telephony, messaging, email, CRM, scheduling, calendar connectivity, analytics or AI functionality. BookedRadar does not publish customer lead data as public proof. Estimated opportunity values and confirmed revenue are reported separately.

Call recordings and transcripts

Call recording is off by default in the BookedRadar pilot architecture and remains separate from transcript history. Transcript history may be enabled only for a customer deployment that explicitly approves transcript retention. When enabled, BookedRadar may retain text from the caller and AI assistant for quality, search, continuity and knowledge-gap analysis, subject to the configured retention policy. Audio recording should not be enabled unless applicable disclosure, consent and retention requirements have been intentionally addressed.

Retention and security

BookedRadar aims to retain only information reasonably needed for operations, reporting, support and legal obligations. Call-history and web-chat records are tenant-isolated and subject to configured retention periods. Production credentials and calendar, CRM, messaging or other provider tokens are intended to be stored in protected server-side secret stores, not in public site code.

Your choices

You may ask BookedRadar to stop marketing communications or to update business contact information. Customer-specific service communications may also be governed by the policies of the home-service company you contacted. Where a customer deployment enables transcript history, the participating business is responsible for approving that configuration and any required caller disclosure or consent appropriate to its use and jurisdiction.

Contact

This notice describes the current BookedRadar operating design. Customer deployments may require additional disclosures or terms based on the channels, integrations and jurisdictions used.
