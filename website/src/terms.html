Website information

BookedRadar website content, audits and demos are provided for business evaluation. Illustrative metrics, scenarios and demos are examples and are not representations of actual customer results unless explicitly labeled otherwise.

No revenue guarantee

Estimated opportunity value is diagnostic. Re-engagement, bookings and confirmed revenue depend on factors outside BookedRadar's control, including lead quality, pricing, service capacity, customer decisions, market conditions and the accuracy of connected systems.

Pilot scope

A paid pilot is governed by the specific scope, integrations, enabled channels, provider costs, responsibilities, retention settings and termination terms agreed with the participating business. The base recovery scope may include voice intake, human transfer, approved messaging, web-chat lead capture, CRM follow-up, recovery workflows and RadarProof reporting. Customer systems and customer-provided data remain subject to the customer’s own policies and permissions.

Automation limits

BookedRadar is designed with human escalation, confirmation and fail-closed guardrails. Confirm-only mode collects preferred timing but does not promise or write a live appointment. Live calendar booking or scheduling may be enabled only after the customer separately approves that scope and the applicable calendar or dispatch integration passes readiness and acceptance testing. BookedRadar should not be relied on as emergency services, professional technical diagnosis, legal advice or a substitute for qualified personnel in hazardous situations.

Human transfers

Calls are transferred to the business’s designated phone. An unanswered or declined call may reach that phone’s voicemail. A transfer does not guarantee a human answer, and the current transfer path does not automatically return the caller to the AI. The business should approve its receiving number and voicemail greeting before activation.

Scheduling and dispatch

Full-time scheduling or dispatch is a separate commercial scope from the founding recovery pilot. It may require customer-specific job durations, technician or crew capabilities, service territories, travel-time and buffer rules, priority logic, cancellation/reschedule rules, capacity limits, connected dispatch systems and a separate agreement reference. Until those requirements are approved and tested, BookedRadar remains confirm-only.

Acceptable use

BookedRadar must not be configured to impersonate people, misrepresent pricing, availability or confirmed appointments, collect unnecessary sensitive information, bypass consent or opt-out requirements, or send communications contrary to applicable channel rules. Customer-specific transcript retention, recording, live scheduling and messaging features must remain disabled unless their required approvals and integrations are in place.

Third-party services

Telephony, messaging, email, CRM, calendar, scheduling, AI, hosting and other integrations may be provided by third parties and may have separate fees, availability limits, authorization requirements and terms. BookedRadar cannot guarantee the continuous availability or behavior of an outside provider.
