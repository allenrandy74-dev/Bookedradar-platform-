09 / STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Know what happens
before you commit.

Is this just an AI receptionist?+

Call handling is one part of BookedRadar. The platform brings together structured intake, human escalation, approved recovery workflows and evidence of outcomes.

Does BookedRadar replace office staff?+

BookedRadar supports your team with repetitive intake and follow-up. Unusual, high-value and human-requested conversations need people. Human staffing is not included.

Can callers reach a person?+

Yes. Callers can ask for a person. Before activation, we configure and test your human-escalation route and agree what happens when someone cannot answer.

Does BookedRadar identify itself as a virtual assistant?+

Our live demos do. Customer wording is configured transparently and tested before activation.

Will it book every lead automatically?+

No. Confirm-only is the default: capture the requested timing for your team to confirm. Live calendar booking needs a separate approved scope, connected availability and acceptance testing. Technician dispatch is custom scoped.

What happens during the Proof Pilot?+

We agree on either after-hours or overflow coverage, configure your rules, and test with your team before real traffic. The evaluation ends at 14 days or 25 real calls, whichever comes first, followed by a RadarProof review.

Can I stop the pilot?+

Yes. We document how to restore your previous call flow before activation. There is no automatic paid conversion or automatic scope expansion.

How is ROI or value measured?+

RadarProof separates captured opportunities, engagement, bookings or acceptances, recovery and confirmed revenue. Estimated opportunity value is not confirmed revenue. Incomplete and failed outcomes belong in the review too.

What happens if an integration fails?+

We test required integrations before activation. Material handoff failures can pause a pilot for review. We investigate, preserve the evidence and use the agreed fallback instead of claiming a failed delivery succeeded.

How quickly can we go live?+

Timing depends on your workflow, integrations and completion of acceptance testing. We start with a narrow scope, collect your business rules, verify the human handoff and obtain your approval before forwarding real traffic.
