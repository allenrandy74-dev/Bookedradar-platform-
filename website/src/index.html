FAQ

Straight answers.

Is this just an AI receptionist?

No. Call handling can be one input, but the core system is broader: detecting leakage, recovering demand, applying booking guardrails, escalating exceptions and showing measurable outcomes.

Does BookedRadar replace office staff?

The design goal is not wholesale staff replacement. It is to automate repetitive follow-up and triage while handing unusual or high-value situations to people.

Will it book every lead automatically?

No. Booking logic should respect service area, urgency, job type, capacity, hours and escalation rules. Some cases should intentionally stop and hand off.

How will ROI be measured?

RadarProof can track the path from source to automated touch to reply to booking to opportunity value. Confirmed revenue should only be labeled as confirmed when the CRM or job system supports that attribution.
