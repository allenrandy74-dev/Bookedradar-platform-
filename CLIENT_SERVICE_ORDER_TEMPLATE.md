## 10. Pricing, billing and cancellation

Service is billed monthly in advance unless otherwise stated in the signed order.

Preferred payment method is ACH; card may be available.

For Founding Partners, the setup fee is waived as shown above. A Founding Partner promotional monthly rate applies while the applicable founding subscription remains continuously active, unless the signed order states otherwise.

Unless a different term is written here, service is **month-to-month**. Either party may elect not to renew. Customer cancellation normally becomes effective at the end of the current paid billing period. Amounts already paid for a current service period are not automatically prorated or refunded after service for that period has begun, except where required by law or expressly agreed in writing.

BookedRadar may pause new outbound automation or service activation when payment is unresolved, while avoiding abrupt unsafe changes to active customer call routing. Any suspension/termination handling should follow the signed agreement and an orderly handoff plan.

## 11. Fair-use and unusually high volume

Published package pricing does not currently promise unlimited call, message, AI, storage, integration, or provider usage.

During early Founding Partner deployments, BookedRadar will monitor real operating costs. BookedRadar will not impose surprise overage charges. If a customer’s volume or integration complexity materially exceeds the agreed package assumptions, BookedRadar and the customer will review a revised scope or price before new recurring overage charges are imposed.

## 12. Customer responsibilities

Customer agrees to:
- provide accurate business hours, services, service area, safety rules and escalation contacts;
- keep BookedRadar informed when those rules change;
- maintain required access to customer-owned phone, CRM, calendar or dispatch systems;
- make qualified personnel available for safety-sensitive or escalated situations;
- avoid instructing BookedRadar to misrepresent pricing, availability, credentials or policies;
- provide any customer-specific notices/consents required for the customer’s business and workflows;
- promptly report incorrect routing, customer-impacting errors or material integration changes.

## 13. BookedRadar responsibilities

BookedRadar will:
- configure only the services included and approved for the customer;
- keep provider-dependent capabilities gated until their activation tests pass;
- use human escalation and fail-closed behavior for unsupported/ambiguous actions where designed;
- separate estimated opportunity value from confirmed revenue;
- use tenant-isolated configuration and credentials;
- provide reasonable assistance correcting service-impacting configuration issues;
- avoid enabling an unaccepted feature merely because it is included in a higher package.

## 14. No guarantee of business results

BookedRadar helps capture, route, recover and measure business opportunities. It does not guarantee revenue, bookings, lead quality, customer decisions, technician availability, successful estimates, financing approval, or any other business outcome.
